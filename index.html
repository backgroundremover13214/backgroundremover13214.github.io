Free Background Remover

Remove the background from an image and create a transparent PNG directly in your browser.

Use this free online background remover to remove the background from an image and create a clean, transparent background in just a few steps.

The tool supports PNG, JPG and WebP images. After removing the image background, you can download a transparent PNG or replace the background with white, black or a custom color.

Background removal runs directly in your browser, so you can quickly prepare product photos, profile pictures, graphics and other images without creating an account.

How it works

STEP 1
Upload
Choose a PNG, JPG or WebP image from your device.
STEP 2
Remove
The tool identifies the main subject and removes the image background.
STEP 3
Download
Download a transparent PNG or choose a new background color.

Frequently asked questions

Is the background remover free?
Yes. The background removal tool can be used without creating an account.
Do I need to create an account?
No. This version of the tool does not require registration or a user account.
What image formats are supported?
The upload tool currently accepts PNG, JPEG and WebP images.
Can I download a transparent image?
Yes. Select Transparent and download the result as a PNG.
Are my images uploaded to a server?
Your selected image is processed locally in your browser by this tool rather than intentionally uploaded to an application server for processing.
Can I change the background after removing it?
Yes. Keep the background transparent or choose white, black or a custom color before downloading the finished image.
